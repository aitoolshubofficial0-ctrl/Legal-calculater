About Our Tool

Legal Settlement Estimator is a free, web-based tool designed to help individuals quickly estimate potential personal injury claim payouts and legal fee breakdowns.

Our mission is to bring transparency to the legal settlement estimation process by using standard industry multipliers (economic and non-economic damages) so users can understand their claim value before consulting with insurance adjusters or attorneys.

We continuously update our formulas to reflect standard legal practices and provide an intuitive, instant calculator experience.
