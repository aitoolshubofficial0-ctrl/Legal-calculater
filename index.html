How Personal Injury Settlements Are Calculated

When estimating a personal injury settlement, insurance adjusters typically divide damages into two main categories: economic damages (tangible expenses like medical bills and lost wages) and non-economic damages (pain and suffering).

Understanding the Multiplier Method

The multiplier method multiplies your total economic damages by a factor ranging between 1.5 and 5, depending on the severity of the injury, recovery duration, and impact on daily life.
