How Injury Settlement Calculations Work

When calculating personal injury claims, insurance companies calculate two distinct types of losses: Economic Damages and Non-Economic Damages.

1. Economic Damages

These represent clear financial losses resulting directly from the injury. Common examples include medical bills, physical therapy fees, prescription costs, lost earnings, and property damage repairs.

2. Non-Economic Damages (The Multiplier)

Non-economic damages compensate for pain, discomfort, mental anguish, and emotional distress. Since pain cannot be directly measured, insurance claims use a multiplier method—typically multiplying total medical costs by a factor between 1.5x and 5.0x.
